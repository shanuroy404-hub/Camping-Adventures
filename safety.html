Before the Trek

1. Check the Weather

Check forecasts, warnings and trail conditions before leaving.

2. Choose the Right Trail

Select a route suitable for your experience and fitness. Follow local rules and guide instructions.

3. Carry Essential Supplies

Bring enough water, food, suitable clothing and a first-aid kit.

4. Stay With Your Group

Follow the guide and avoid wandering away from the group or marked trail.

5. Protect Nature

Do not litter, disturb wildlife or enter restricted areas.

6. Know Emergency Procedures

Save local emergency contacts and tell someone your itinerary. Turn back if conditions become unsafe.
